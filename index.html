05. Blog & Research

Current Thesis
2025 – 2026 Research Thesis

Beyond Catastrophic Forgetting: Orthogonally Projected Weight Editing in Protein Language Models

Large biological foundation models such as Protein Language Models (pLMs) are revolutionizing computational biology and drug discovery. However, editing or fine-tuning them for safety and specific downstream utility frequently triggers catastrophic forgetting of core evolutionary representations.

Under the supervision of Saklain Abdullah (Lecturer, East Delta University), my undergraduate research investigates Orthogonally Projected Weight Editing. By projecting weight updates onto subspaces orthogonal to the gradients of general biological utilities, we ensure edits do not disrupt pretrained representations — dominating the safety–utility Pareto frontier.
